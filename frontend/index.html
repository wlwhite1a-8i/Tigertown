Tiger Town: Explore Jackson, MS helps the Jackson State community find local restaurants, entertainment, shopping, parks, and study spots in Jackson and nearby cities.

The starting catalog comes from 40 anonymous survey responses. We researched the suggested places and kept source links so you can check the details before heading out.
